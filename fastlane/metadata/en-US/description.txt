SimpleTimer+ gives you three independent timers on a single screen - no tabs, no clutter, no setup.

Each timer can be set as a countdown or used as a stopwatch. Pick your minutes and seconds with the wheel picker, choose from 16 built-in alarm sounds, and enable loop mode to restart automatically.

Features:
- 3 independent timers on one screen
- Countdown or stopwatch mode per timer
- Wheel picker for quick time selection
- 16 selectable iOS alarm sounds
- Loop mode - auto-restart when finished
- Timer log with timestamps and total time
- Light, Dark, or System appearance
- Supports English and German
